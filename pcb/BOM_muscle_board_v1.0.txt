I2C ADDRESS CONFIGURATION:
  Solder jumpers on PB0-PB2 set address 0x20-0x27
  Internal pull-ups enabled in firmware (no external resistors needed)
  Default (all open) = 0x27 (all pulled HIGH)
  Short A0 to GND = 0x26, short A0+A1 = 0x24, etc.

I2C PULL-UP CONFIGURATION:
  R3 (SDA) and R4 (SCL) are 4.7K pull-ups to +5V.
  Enable by bridging solder jumper pads near R3/R4.
  Only enable on the LAST cell in the I2C chain.
  All other cells: leave jumper pads open.
