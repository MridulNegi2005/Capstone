KOBRA 2 NEO / 0.4 NOZZLE / NUMAKERS PETG-HS 1.75 mm
ONE combined job: ~9 h 33 min / 108 g. Have at least 150 g filament.

Nozzle: 240 C first layer, then 235 C
Bed: 80 C throughout
Layer: 0.16 mm; first layer 0.24 mm
Walls: 5; top/bottom: 6 layers; top minimum thickness 1 mm
Infill: 40% crosshatch
Supports / raft / ironing: OFF
Brim: 8 mm outer; 0.15 mm gap (some brims may join)
Speed mm/s: first 20; outer 40; inner 60; infill 80; top 30; bridge 25
Acceleration mm/s2: first 500; outer 800; normal 1000; travel 1500
Retraction: 1 mm at 35 mm/s; Z-hop 0.2 mm
Fan: OFF first 3 layers; 30-50% later; bridges 50%
Flow: 100%; volumetric limit: 8 mm3/s (uncalibrated starting settings)
Orca hole compensation: +0.10 mm radial
Scale: 100%, MILLIMETRES. Keep flat. Print BY LAYER.

The start G-code runs Auto Level (G29) by itself before every print. Verify the first layer.
Use owner's suitable textured PEI. Smooth PEI needs PETG separation layer.
Dry wet PETG with controlled dryer per spool guidance; keep sealed afterwards.
Do not auto-orient, scale, enable supports or use sequential/by-object mode.
These settings are shared by ALL parts. No different wall settings required.

FIRST LAYER FIX (the last prints had bad bottoms and good tops)
Cause: the nozzle was too high on the first layers. The lines did not press into the bed.
Do these steps before the print:
1. Let the bed cool. Clean the sheet with isopropyl alcohol and a clean tissue. Do not touch it after.
2. Remove old plastic from the nozzle tip. The nozzle touches the bed during Auto Level.
3. Start the print. The printer heats the bed, then taps the bed at many points (Auto Level).
   This takes a few minutes. Do not touch the printer during this time.
   If the printer does not tap the bed, it uses the last saved Auto Level.
   In that case, run Auto Level from the printer menu one time, then start the print again.
4. Watch the first minute after Auto Level (the purge line and the brim).
5. Look at the lines. Good lines are flat and touch each other with no gaps.
6. If the lines are round, or you see gaps between them, the nozzle is too high.
   Use Z-offset (baby-step) during the print. Lower it by 0.05 mm. Wait 10 seconds. Look again.
7. If the lines are very thin, rough, or the nozzle scrapes the bed, the nozzle is too low.
   Raise it by 0.05 mm.
8. Stop when the lines are flat. Do not change other settings.
9. Write down the Z-offset value. Use it for the next print.

STRINGS AND HAIRS: wet PETG causes them. Dry the spool at 65 C for 4 to 6 hours before the print.

FINGER SHEETS: print them flat, ridges up, as in the STL. Do not turn them over.
The bending lines must run along each tongue. Check this in the Orca preview.
